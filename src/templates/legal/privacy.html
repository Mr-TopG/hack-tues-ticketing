Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 July 2026

This policy explains how the Hack TUES Tickets platform at tickets.mrtopg.org ("we", "us", or "the platform") handles personal information when you create an account, register for an event, use a ticket, or manage an event.

Information we collect

  • Account information, including your name, email address, password credentials in hashed form, and email-verification status.
  • If you use Google login, your Google account identifier, verified email address, and basic profile information such as your name. We do not store Google access or refresh tokens.
  • Ticket records, including the event and category, issue and cancellation times, ticket-delivery state, and a private QR validation token.
  • Check-in records, including when a ticket was checked in and the authorized account that performed the check-in.
  • Organizer-request and event-management information, including organization name and the reason supplied for requesting organizer access.
  • Technical and security information such as IP address, browser information, request time, and limited server logs. QR validation tokens are redacted from production access logs.

How we use information

We use this information to:

  • create, secure, and support user accounts;
  • allocate tickets fairly and prevent overselling;
  • generate, deliver, validate, and cancel tickets;
  • review organizer requests and operate events;
  • detect abuse and investigate technical problems;
  • send essential account and ticket messages; and
  • meet applicable legal and record-keeping duties.

Cookies

The platform uses necessary session, authentication, and CSRF-protection cookies. These cookies keep you signed in and protect forms from unauthorized submissions. We do not currently use advertising or analytics cookies.

Service providers

We share only the information needed to operate the service with infrastructure and authentication providers. These currently include Cloudflare for secure traffic delivery and Google when you choose Google login. An email-delivery provider may process your email address when transactional email is enabled.

Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information.

Storage and retention

Application data and private ticket files are stored on infrastructure controlled for this platform. We keep information only as long as reasonably necessary to operate events, protect the service, resolve disputes, and satisfy legal obligations.

You can request account deletion from the Account page. Some ticket and check-in records may need to be retained for event security, fraud prevention, or audit purposes. The platform will explain when those records prevent immediate deletion.

Your choices and rights

You can update your name, manage connected accounts, cancel an eligible ticket, or request account deletion through the platform. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to access, correct, restrict, export, or object to the processing of your personal information.

Security

We use HTTPS, access controls, private ticket storage, hashed passwords, and opaque QR tokens. No online system can guarantee absolute security, so keep your account and ticket QR code private and contact us if you suspect misuse.

Minors

If you are below the age at which you can consent to this processing under applicable law, use the platform only with permission from a parent or legal guardian.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the platform or legal requirements change. Material updates will be identified by a new effective date.

For privacy questions or requests, contact tickets@mrtopg.org .
